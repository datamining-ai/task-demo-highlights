## Bridge certificate file

The facility is 350.0, with 200.0 borrowed on 18 July, leaving **nominal** undrawn capacity of 150.0. Section 2.01 permits up to three borrowing occasions and prohibits reborrowing repaid amounts; the supplied record identifies one draw. Section 2.03 requires notice by noon New York time two business days before borrowing. Section 4.02 requires the borrowing request, applicable representations, no continuing Default/Event of Default, specified post-closing basket conditions, and payment of applicable fees/expenses. Treasury must verify these matters and any commitment reductions. No certificate should be signed merely because the arithmetic capacity exists.

Sections 3.11 and 5.08 limit proceeds to general corporate purposes excluding purchase, repayment or redemption of **Indebtedness**, and specified financing transaction fees/expenses. The agreement's defined term matters: section 1.01 excludes qualifying Advance Payments from Indebtedness except following a defined Discontinuance Event. The definition of Advance Payment requires the specified connection to future deliveries/services/expenditures; a customer label or financial-statement financing classification alone is insufficient. The calendar-date Boeing MOA repayments warrant a documented classification review. Obtain Treasury/legal or lender confirmation before relying on bridge proceeds for the queued Boeing repayment; no classification opinion or special waiver is held by this office.
